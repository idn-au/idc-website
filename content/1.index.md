::Statement
The Indigenous Data Commons is an online platform developed by the Indigenous Data Network at the University of Melbourne. The website provides access to the Indigenous Data Catalogue, a directory and metadata catalogue of Indigenous data assets, by, about or including Indigenous data held across Australia. The online platform also supports and empowers Indigenous data custodians and stewards, to record, curate and update their data sources, and to discover Indigenous data created and held by other organisations, institutions, government agencies or people. 
::
